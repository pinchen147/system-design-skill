# Archetype router

Routing index for [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), read at **Run and challenge the envelope**. Select by the invariant the design must protect, not by a familiar product name. Read only the matching pack after the envelope and invariants identify it; compose packs only when independent invariants require it. Reusable mechanisms and decision ladders remain in [HEURISTICS.md](HEURISTICS.md).

Each pack's archetypes retain a **Not when** disqualifier and an **Anti-gate** below which their machinery is unjustified. Apply those before importing any moves or staff gates.

| Protected invariant | Load this pack | Routing signals | Not when | Anti-gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A scarce claim, ordered mutation, or authoritative record is accepted once and remains correct under contention | [Authoritative state](archetypes/authoritative-state.md) | reservations, ledgers, single-writer order, replicated authority, monotonic identity | The data is a disposable or rebuildable view of another authority | One transactional authority already meets the measured contention, availability, and recovery envelope |
| A derived answer remains useful under freshness, ranking, fan-out, locality, or ingest-cost pressure | [Derived serving](archetypes/derived-serving.md) | feeds, indexes, online decisions, spatial retrieval, rollups, multi-tenant SQL | The answer itself is the authoritative mutation path | The existing datastore's index or cache meets the measured latency, freshness, and volume envelope |
| Concurrent clients converge, reconnect, and resume without losing accepted local or connected state | [Realtime and sync](archetypes/realtime-sync.md) | connections, collaborative documents, offline-first clients, mutable namespace sync, conferencing | One writer can be authoritative and clients need neither offline work nor connection state | Polling, a visible lock, or upload/download with a version column satisfies the product contract |
| Accepted work finishes inside its deadline or survives retries without duplicate effects | [Traffic and work](archetypes/traffic-work.md) | admission, quotas, workflows, crawling, third-party delivery, sandboxed execution | Work is neither overload-sensitive nor longer-lived than one request | A bounded queue, timeout, and idempotent handler cover the measured peak and failure window |
| Authority, policy, identity, or key material remains bounded and revocable across failure zones | [Control and security](archetypes/control-security.md) | rate limits, control planes, sessions, revocation, encryption keys, AI tool authority | The value is ordinary application data with no trust-boundary or fleet-wide control role | One deployable and one trust boundary can use local sessions, configuration, and managed encryption directly |
| Immutable or large bytes arrive verifiably and are distributed without mutable-name ambiguity | [Data delivery](archetypes/data-delivery.md) | media, artifacts, edge delivery, crowd relay | The payload is small, mutable, personalized state | One origin behind a commodity CDN or one registry satisfies the measured egress and trust envelope |

Specialized requests are routing signals, not archetypes that bypass the gates: video conferencing starts with **Stateful connection fabric**; sandboxed elastic execution with **Admission control and quota** plus **Durable workflow and retrying work DAG**; multi-tenant SQL with authoritative state for mutations or derived serving for read locality; crowd relay with data delivery when sightings are immutable payloads; and AI application layers with the pack protecting the tool or data invariant at issue. Apply the selected entries' **Not when** and **Anti-gate** rather than treating the signal as a preselected solution.
