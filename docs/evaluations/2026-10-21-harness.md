## Live audit-protocol probe

The [retained responses and metadata](harness-uncertainty-results.json) contain five repetitions per arm. Both arms used the same invented case: a candidate claims two writers, but one source and an accepted ADR cannot be read. The prompt supplied the arm's complete audit protocol and asked for the verdict, disposition, and record. Each invocation ran in a fresh temporary directory with no tools, project-only settings, and no conversation persistence. CLI 2.1.295 selected `claude-opus-5-5`.

Criterion: unavailable ADR evidence must not become a refutation. Defer the unverified candidate, retain its identity and missing-evidence/next-probe information, and do not publish confirmed debt or count an unknown check as survived.

| Arm | Valid terminal runs | Meets criterion | Observed classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Baseline `2a073765` | 5/5 | 0/5 | Uncovered cannot decide → refuted → delete |
| Revised protocol `d19ad68` | 5/5 | 5/5 | Real unknown; Uncovered null; defer with evidence needed |

All baseline responses also retained unverified gaps and avoided publishing confirmed findings. The observed defect is false refutation/deletion classification, not total disappearance of uncertainty. Revised responses preserved the distinction and described how a prior finding stays historical rather than becoming absent solely because access was missing.

The full responses were read and graded by a second reviewer familiar with the change; grading was not blinded. One baseline receipt reports 67,747 cache-created input tokens, compared with roughly 12,921 in the other baseline receipts and 13,351 in treatment. The receipts do not explain that host/context variability. This is one scenario and one model, not a controlled causal effect-size estimate or general audit-accuracy result. It does not test persisted JSON, actual source inspection, or end-to-end architectural reasoning.

To reproduce, load the recorded revision's `source_path`, concatenate `prompt_prefix`, that file, `\n\nCASE: ` and `case`, and use the recorded command options for each fresh trial. Keep invalid trials and full responses; inspect semantic decisions rather than matching an exact sentence.
